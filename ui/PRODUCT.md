# RoomSmith — Product Truth

## Audience

One to four co-located players ages 8+ with adult supervision for children. The Fire TV is the shared story surface; an Android companion is a setup and observation tool, not a second game dashboard.

## Core job

**Understand the shared puzzle, act with physical objects or an accessible fallback, and see the world respond reliably.**

## Operating context

- Ten-foot cinematic Fire TV experience with remote-only, solo, no-timing, captions, and reduced-motion paths.
- Variable rooms, lighting, camera positions, and network quality; perception failures must feel recoverable rather than punitive.
- Cooperative play with different reading, motor, vision, and speech needs.

## Product personality

Atmospheric, nautical, inventive, and reassuring. A midnight lighthouse adventure built from practical household objects—not a horror escape room, surveillance console, or generic fantasy card game.

## Voice

- `Place the red lens to the left of the archive book.`
- `Hold still… alignment found.`
- `Camera lost the mat. Continue with the remote or recalibrate.`
- `The story remembers your solution path.`

Avoid: `AI is watching`, scary surveillance language, ridicule after failure, or flashing alarms.

## Durable constraints

- Story art supports the puzzle but never hides the current instruction or focus.
- Verification feedback is stable and deliberate, not a flicker of every detection frame.
- Camera fallback is available within two actions and preserves narrative progress.
- Companion setup exposes contours only for adult calibration; normal play is quiet.
- Case reports use game-state illustrations, never room images.

## Visual anti-references

- Generic blue enterprise interfaces, glassmorphic HUD clutter, and neon outlines on every object.
- Horror imagery, jump scares, flashing alarms, and red failure screens.
- Public webcams, face boxes, emotion labels, and room-snapshot sharing.
- Tiny remote targets and cinematic text over busy artwork without a scrim.

## Design success signals

- Players can identify the current puzzle, object roles, and next action from across the room.
- A detection failure leads to a clear recovery without breaking group momentum.
- Focus, captions, and verification remain readable over every scene.
- The magic comes from physical-to-scene causality, not constant visual effects.
